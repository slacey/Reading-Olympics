Individual scoring

Readers earn points by completing challenge books. The MVP intentionally stays simple so families can understand it immediately.

10 points × books completed, capped at 100

Country scoring

We normalize by active participants, rather than total population, so countries with very different audience sizes can compete fairly.

Country score = total reader points ÷ active readers

Completion rate

The share of active readers who finish all 10 books is displayed as a second measure of team achievement and is used as the tie-breaker.

Finishers ÷ active readers × 100

Eligibility

A minimum active-reader threshold keeps a single reader from making a country appear to dominate the leaderboard. The default prototype threshold is 5.

Eligible when active readers ≥ threshold
